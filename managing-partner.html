Profile

Professional advice with a practical lens.

Mercy Barasa is an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya, founder of Mercy Barasa & Co. Advocates and a Certified Professional Mediator. She completed her Bachelor of Laws at Moi University and the Advocates Training Programme at the Kenya School of Law.

Her professional background includes legal-assistant and associate-advocate roles before private practice, with work across civil litigation, landlord and tenant disputes, contracts, negotiations, property matters and alternative dispute resolution.
