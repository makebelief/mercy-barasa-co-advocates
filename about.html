About the firm

Practical legal support from Nairobi.

Mercy Barasa & Co. Advocates is a Nairobi legal practice established in 2017, providing legal counsel, representation and dispute-resolution services from AppleWood Adams on Ngong Road, Nairobi.

Counsel shaped around the decision in front of you.

The practice serves individual and business clients across civil disputes, landlord and tenant matters, mediation, property work, commercial advisory and document authentication services.

The firm is led by Mercy Barasa, an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya and Certified Professional Mediator, combining formal legal representation with alternative dispute-resolution capability.
