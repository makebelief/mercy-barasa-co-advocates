Insights

Questions worth asking early.

General information only. Legal advice should be based on the particular facts and documents of your matter.

Dispute Resolution

Before a dispute escalates

Preserve contracts, messages, receipts, notices and other records. The available evidence can shape negotiation, mediation and litigation strategy.

Mediation

Consider whether the dispute can be resolved without trial

Mediation can provide a structured forum for identifying interests, narrowing issues and negotiating workable terms where the circumstances permit.

Landlord & Tenant

Start with the lease and the notices

Tenancy disputes often turn on the written lease, payment records, statutory notices and the nature of the premises involved.

Property

Due diligence before commitment

Property transactions should be checked carefully before funds and signed documents move between parties.

Commercial

Contracts should answer difficult questions early

Payment, performance, risk, termination and dispute clauses matter most when a commercial relationship stops going smoothly.

Legal Documents

Authentication depends on the document and intended use

Affidavits, statutory declarations and documents requiring notarisation or commissioning should be prepared and executed using the correct legal process.
